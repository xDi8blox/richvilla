01 The house

Serviced apartments with the ease of a hotel, on a quiet street in Unity Estate.

RichVilla is a serene, budget-friendly apartment hotel at 28 Omu-Aran Street, Egbeda. Each apartment has its own fitted kitchen, air-conditioning and free Wi-Fi, with a front desk, an on-site restaurant and free, secure parking downstairs.

It is about fifteen minutes’ drive from the local and international airports in Ikeja, which makes it an easy base for a weekend, a family visit or a longer stay in Lagos. As the house puts it: a touch of royalty.
